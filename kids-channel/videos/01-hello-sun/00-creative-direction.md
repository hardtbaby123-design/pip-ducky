Scene cuts land on musical phrase boundaries (see production spec).

### 2. Motion — pure ffmpeg zoom/pan + beat-synced timing ONLY
No AI-animated clip in v1. The keyframes are strong and stylistically consistent; any AI clip risks style drift and breaks the flat high-contrast language. Revisit controlled AI motion only after the visual system is proven.

### 3. Audio — instrumental music-box ONLY
- C major, 92 BPM, sine + harmonics with decay envelopes, soft shaker.
- **No vocals in v1** — not even hummed. We don't fake vocals.
- The "chorus looped 7x with zero variation" idea is **vetoed**. Arrangement must evolve: layer changes per scene (shaker intensity, harmonic additions, bass entry/exit, octave doubling in finale, slight ritardando at the close).

### 4. Title (FINAL, non-negotiable)
**Hello Sun! 🌞 | Colorful Shapes Dance Baby Sensory**

### 5. Quality bar (must ALL clear before upload)
- Every cut lands on a beat.
- High contrast holds on phone screens at 50% brightness.
- A parent with a 6-month-old feels it "holds" attention for the full 2:45.
- Zero visible drift between motion and music.
- Thumbnail + title look like the same family as the video.
- If it feels 80% there, we don't upload. We fix it.
